Behind every report

Reliable costing, professional inspection, AI-assisted analysis and a designated, insured professional

Reliable costing

Cost estimates draw on our database of comparable strata buildings, hands-on experience and recognized contractor cost sources, so your forecast reflects what work really costs.

Professional inspection

The appraiser who signs your report inspects every component in person and records its condition with photographs.

AI-assisted analysis

AI organizes your records and speeds up analysis, so you get your report sooner: inspection usually within three weeks, draft about four weeks later. Every figure is checked by the appraiser.

Designated and insured

Signed by an AACI and MRICS designated member, backed by professional liability insurance. An AACI is a qualified person under section 94 of the Strata Property Act and section 6.2 of the Strata Property Regulation.

Easy to read

Plain words, clear charts and guided notes that walk council through each section. No more wrestling with equations or formulas.

Competitive fixed fees

Townhouse from $2,000, low-rise from $3,000, high-rise from $5,000, agreed before we start.
