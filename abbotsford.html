Deadlines for Abbotsford stratas · Fraser Valley

July 1, 2026

Abbotsford is in the Fraser Valley Regional District. Stratas here with no report, or a last report dated before December 31, 2020, had to have a report by this date. If yours is overdue, we can usually inspect within three weeks.

Every 5 years

After that, every strata corporation with five or more lots needs a new depreciation report at least every five years. The ¾ vote to waive it ended on July 1, 2024.

New stratas

Stratas formed from July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2027 need a first report within two years of the first AGM. From July 1, 2027, within 18 months.

Deadlines differ by region in BC. See the deadline for each region. General information under the Strata Property Act and Regulation, not legal advice.

What we look at closely in Abbotsford

Every component is inspected in person. These are the items that most often shape Abbotsford forecasts.

Fraser Valley weather

Abbotsford sees hotter summers and sharper cold snaps than the coast. We reflect what we see on roofs, paving and exteriors in their remaining lives.

Drainage and site risk

Recent Fraser Valley floods have councils asking about drainage. We record visible drainage issues and note where a specialist review may be worth getting.

Bare land stratas

The report covers roads, utilities, amenities and other common assets only, not owners' homes.

Self-managed councils

Many Abbotsford stratas are self-managed. We can gather missing records for you with council's authorization.

Abbotsford depreciation report questions

General information under the Strata Property Act and Regulation as of November 2026, not legal advice. More questions

When is our Abbotsford strata's depreciation report due?

Abbotsford is in the Fraser Valley Regional District. If your strata has no report, or its last report is dated before December 31, 2020, it was due by July 1, 2026. After that, a new report is needed at least every five years.

How much does a depreciation report cost in Abbotsford?

The same fixed fees as everywhere we work: from $2,000 for a townhouse, $3,000 for a low-rise and $5,000 for a high-rise, before GST. Complete records lower the fee.

Do you inspect Abbotsford properties in person?

Yes. The appraiser who signs your report inspects the property in person, usually within three weeks of your deposit.
